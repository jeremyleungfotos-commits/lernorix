## europe-alliances-1914.svg
- Title: Map Europe alliances 1914-en.
- Authors: historicair (French original), Fluteflute and Bibi Saint-Pol (English translation).
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Map_Europe_alliances_1914-en.svg
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.5, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.5/
- Unmodified original. Shows prewar alliances, including Italy in the Triple Alliance; not wartime membership in 1915 or 1916. Country labels and legend supplement colors; learners can enlarge the map.
